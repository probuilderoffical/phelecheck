# PheleCheck Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 21, 2026_

PheleCheck helps users assess fraud and scam risk. This policy describes the data PheleCheck processes in the current release.

## Information you choose to submit

PheleCheck processes the messages, links, phone/payment context, screenshots, or QR images that you choose to check. Do not submit passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs, CVV codes, full payment-card numbers, private keys, seed phrases, or content you are not permitted to share.

Screenshots and QR images are sent for the current analysis and are not stored by PheleCheck as uploaded files. Check history stores a short label/preview and the analysis result, not the raw screenshot file.

## Accounts, history, and preferences

If you create an account, PheleCheck may store your email address, app preferences, check results, and optional memory/history data so they can be available across sessions. You can turn local history and memory off and clear local history/memory from Settings.

## AI and web-evidence processing

PheleCheck currently uses a Cloudflare-hosted AI model for fraud-risk analysis. Link checks may also use Cloudflare URL Scanner evidence. These services receive the content necessary to perform the check.

The absence of a malicious web record does not mean a website is safe.

## Improving PheleCheck

"Improve PheleCheck" is off by default for new users. If you enable it, reviewed and de-identified metadata from checks may be considered for future model improvement. New training candidates do not retain a direct user ID or a link to the source check.

PheleCheck does not automatically retrain its model on every submission.

## Scam reports

Reports submitted through the Report feature are stored for review. Reports do not automatically change reputation or train the model.

## Security and abuse prevention

PheleCheck uses access controls, row-level database security, and server-side rate limiting to reduce abuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention

Raw uploaded screenshot/QR files are not stored by PheleCheck. Account history and reports may remain until deleted under applicable product controls or retention procedures. Additional deletion/export controls may be introduced as the service matures.

## Automated-risk limitations

PheleCheck gives risk guidance, not a guarantee. A Low Risk result does not prove legitimacy, and a High Risk result is not a declaration that a person or company is criminal. Important payments and identities should be verified independently through official channels.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as PheleCheck adds features, providers, or legal/compliance requirements. Material changes should be reflected in the app and this policy before they take effect.
