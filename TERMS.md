# PheleCheck Terms & Safety Notice

_Last updated: September 21, 2026_

PheleCheck is an automated fraud-risk assistance tool. It is designed to help users notice suspicious signals and make more informed verification decisions.

## Not a guarantee

PheleCheck cannot guarantee that any person, message, website, account, payment, QR code, or transaction is safe or fraudulent. Results may be incomplete or wrong.

A Low Risk result is not proof of safety. A High Risk result is a warning based on detected evidence, not an accusation or legal finding.

## User responsibility

Before sending money or sharing sensitive information, independently verify the recipient or organization using official contact details you find yourself. PheleCheck should not be the sole basis for a financial, legal, law-enforcement, employment, or identity decision.

## Sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, OTPs, PINs, CVV codes, full card numbers, private keys, seed phrases, or other secrets. Do not submit content you do not have permission to share.

## Availability

AI, web-evidence, network, and third-party services may be unavailable, delayed, or incomplete. PheleCheck may provide limited local analysis when cloud analysis cannot be reached.

## Reports and model improvement

User reports are reviewed before they can affect product decisions. PheleCheck does not automatically train its model on every user submission.

## Changes

These terms and safety notices may be updated as the product, providers, and applicable requirements change.
